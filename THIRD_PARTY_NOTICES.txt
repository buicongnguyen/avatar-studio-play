AVATAR STUDIO — DANCE PREVIEW
Third-party notices and asset/motion credits

MODEL AND ART PROVENANCE

The upgraded avatars were built locally by the 3d_body_dancer project
(Claude, with Codex face/jaw refinement on the separately labeled candidate)
from MakeHuman CC0 assets using MPFB and Blender. The project adapts the
human mesh, skin/eye textures, eyebrows, eyelashes, hair and mouth assets,
and supplies fitted garments, materials, morphs, rig adaptations and edits.

MakeHuman Community: https://www.makehumancommunity.org/
MPFB: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/
CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The output GLB asset.copyright notice is retained where supplied:
"Avatar built by 3d_body_dancer (Claude) from MakeHuman CC0 assets via MPFB;
clips generated by Mini_game_dancing; see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt"

Blender and MPFB are authoring tools, not included software in this static
website. Their software licenses are separate from the CC0 asset provenance
recorded by the model build. Reference character images were generated for
this project as visual design targets. The final 3D likeness is an artistic
adaptation, not a photographic reconstruction.

Original Nabi and Haru starter characters and the procedural dance/gameplay
clips originate in the owner's Mini_game_dancing project. Those procedural
clips were generated or regenerated for the avatar skeleton. They are
separate from the third-party retargeted clips credited below. This notice
does not put the whole website or all project-authored work under CC0 or MIT.

MOTION SOURCES AND MODIFICATIONS

Only the selected, retargeted and edited animation clips embedded in the
avatar GLBs are distributed here. The raw ASF, AMC and BVH datasets and source
animation-library downloads are not included. Processing includes skeleton
retargeting, segment selection, scale changes, loop and root-motion edits,
foot placement and contact corrections. The source creators do not endorse
this demo.
